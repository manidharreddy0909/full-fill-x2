# FULFILL-X — ADVERSARIAL RESILIENCE EXPLAINED

> **Location**: `backend/app/adversary/`  

---

## 1. Red-Team Adversary Agent Purpose
The Adversary Agent acts as an automated red-team challenger. Its primary goal is to **attack candidate mitigation plans** proposed by the Specialist Swarm Agents to discover hidden single-point-of-failure vulnerabilities before execution.

---

## 2. Attack Vectors Executed
1. **Secondary Disruption Injection**: Simulates a simultaneous carrier port strike while rerouting inventory.
2. **Capacity Bottleneck Exploitation**: Overloads secondary supplier allocation to test contract limits.
3. **Cascading Latency Attack**: Introduces cumulative shipping delays to break customer SLA commitments.
